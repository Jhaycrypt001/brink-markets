# Naming Decision

## Shortlist

1. **Brink**
2. **Quorum**
3. **Vantage**
4. **Morrow**
5. **Tally**

## Recommendation

Use **Brink** as the working product name and **Brink Markets** as the public-facing name.

It is short, pronounceable, easy to remember, and communicates the moment immediately before an event resolves. It also leaves room for discovery, analytics, embedded market cards, and execution products without trapping the company inside a “prediction” label.

## Naming constraints

- No “AI”, “bot”, “oracle”, “bet”, or “predict” in the primary brand.
- Must work as a noun and a product verb: “Open Brink”, “share a Brink market”, “Brink score”.
- Must support future products: API, widgets, agent tooling, and analytics.
- Domain, trademark, app-store, and social-handle availability must be checked before public launch.
